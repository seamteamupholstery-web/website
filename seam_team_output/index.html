Meet the team

A small shop
in Chichester.

What began as one visit to a local upholsterer quickly turned into rebuilding the seats of a 1958 Chevy Del Rey — a project that sparked something bigger than either of us anticipated: a husband-and-wife shop built around creativity and hands-on craftsmanship.

Every piece is carefully patterned, hand-cut, and professionally sewn in-house and built with purpose for wherever life takes you.

Family-run · Chichester, NH
